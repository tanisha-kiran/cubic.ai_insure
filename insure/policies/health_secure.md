# POLICY | HLT-001 | Northstar HealthSecure Individual (synthetic sample) | health
> Fictional policy written for the cubic.ai Insure demo. Not a real insurer or product.

### [HLT-1.1] | 2 | definition | Definitions
"Waiting period" means the time after the start date during which a benefit is not payable. "Pre-existing condition" means a condition diagnosed or treated within 36 months before the start date. "Hospitalisation" means admission to a hospital as an inpatient for at least 24 consecutive hours.

### [HLT-2.1] | 3 | coverage | In-patient hospitalisation
We pay the cost of room, nursing, doctor fees, surgery, anaesthesia and medicines during hospitalisation, up to the Sum Insured of INR 500,000 per policy year. Room rent is limited to INR 5,000 per day.

### [HLT-2.2] | 3 | coverage | Pre- and post-hospitalisation
We pay medical expenses incurred 30 days before admission and 60 days after discharge if they relate to the same illness or injury.

### [HLT-2.3] | 4 | coverage | Day-care procedures
We pay for listed day-care procedures such as cataract surgery and dialysis, where the procedure takes less than 24 hours because of technological advancement.

### [HLT-2.4] | 4 | coverage | Ambulance
We pay up to INR 2,000 per hospitalisation for road ambulance to the nearest hospital.

### [HLT-2.5] | 4 | coverage | Maternity benefit
Maternity expenses are covered up to INR 50,000 for normal delivery and INR 75,000 for caesarean delivery, after the maternity waiting period of 24 months.

### [HLT-3.1] | 5 | condition | Initial waiting period
No claim is payable for any illness in the first 30 days after the start date. This does not apply to injury caused by an accident.

### [HLT-3.2] | 5 | condition | Specified illness waiting period
Claims for hernia, cataract, joint replacement, kidney stones and piles are payable only after 24 months of continuous cover.

### [HLT-3.3] | 5 | condition | Pre-existing condition waiting period
Pre-existing conditions are covered only after 36 months of continuous cover and only if they were declared on the proposal form.

### [HLT-4.1] | 6 | exclusion | Cosmetic and dental
We do not pay for cosmetic or plastic surgery unless needed after an accident or to treat cancer, nor for dental treatment unless it requires hospitalisation after an accident.

### [HLT-4.2] | 6 | exclusion | Outpatient consultations
We do not pay for outpatient doctor consultations, routine check-ups or vitamins, except as stated in clause 2.2.

### [HLT-4.3] | 6 | exclusion | Self-inflicted and substance related
We do not pay for self-inflicted injury, treatment of alcohol or drug dependence, or injury caused while under the influence of intoxicants.

### [HLT-4.4] | 7 | exclusion | War and hazardous sports
We do not pay for treatment arising from war or for injury during adventure sports such as paragliding, mountaineering and motor racing.

### [HLT-5.1] | 8 | limit | Co-payment
If the Insured Person is aged 61 or above at the start date, a co-payment of 10 percent of each admissible claim applies.

### [HLT-5.2] | 8 | limit | No claim bonus
For each claim-free year the Sum Insured increases by 10 percent, up to a maximum of 50 percent.

### [HLT-6.1] | 9 | claims | Cashless claims
For planned admission, request pre-authorisation at least 48 hours before admission at a network hospital. For emergency admission, inform us within 24 hours.

### [HLT-6.2] | 9 | claims | Reimbursement claims
Submit the claim within 30 days of discharge with the claim form, discharge summary, original bills, prescriptions and investigation reports.

### [HLT-6.3] | 10 | claims | Claim settlement
We aim to settle a complete claim within 30 days of receiving all documents. Interest is payable on delay beyond that period.
