# POLICY | MTR-001 | Northstar DriveShield Comprehensive Car (synthetic sample) | motor
> Fictional policy written for the cubic.ai Insure demo. Not a real insurer or product.

### [MTR-1.1] | 2 | definition | Definitions
"Insured Declared Value (IDV)" means the agreed market value of the vehicle at the start date, shown on the schedule. "Depreciation" means reduction in the value of parts according to the vehicle's age.

### [MTR-2.1] | 3 | coverage | Own damage
We pay for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accident, fire, explosion, theft, flood, earthquake, riot or malicious act, up to the IDV.

### [MTR-2.2] | 3 | coverage | Third-party liability
We cover your legal liability for death or bodily injury to a third party without limit, and for damage to third-party property up to INR 750,000.

### [MTR-2.3] | 4 | coverage | Personal accident cover for owner-driver
We pay INR 1,500,000 on death or permanent total disability of the owner-driver caused by an accident involving the insured vehicle, provided the owner-driver holds a valid driving licence.

### [MTR-2.4] | 4 | coverage | Towing
We pay reasonable towing charges up to INR 3,000 per accident to the nearest repairer.

### [MTR-3.1] | 5 | limit | Compulsory deductible
A compulsory deductible of INR 1,000 applies to each own-damage claim. A voluntary deductible shown on the schedule is applied in addition.

### [MTR-3.2] | 5 | limit | Depreciation on parts
Depreciation is applied to replaced parts as follows: rubber, plastic and batteries at 50 percent, fibreglass at 30 percent, and metal parts according to the vehicle's age. This does not apply where the Zero Depreciation add-on appears on the schedule.

### [MTR-3.3] | 5 | limit | No claim bonus
A no claim bonus of 20 percent applies after one claim-free year, rising to 50 percent after five claim-free years. It is lost if a claim is paid.

### [MTR-4.1] | 6 | exclusion | Unlicensed or intoxicated driver
We do not pay for loss or damage when the vehicle is driven by a person without a valid driving licence or under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

### [MTR-4.2] | 6 | exclusion | Wear and tear and mechanical breakdown
We do not pay for wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, tyre damage unless the vehicle is also damaged at the same time, or damage from continued use after a loss.

### [MTR-4.3] | 6 | exclusion | Consequential loss
We do not pay for loss of use, depreciation in value, or any consequential loss.

### [MTR-4.4] | 7 | exclusion | Water ingress to engine
We do not pay for engine damage caused by water entering the engine after the vehicle is driven through flood water, unless the Engine Protect add-on appears on the schedule.

### [MTR-4.5] | 7 | exclusion | Commercial use and racing
We do not cover the vehicle when used for hire or reward, racing or speed testing.

### [MTR-5.1] | 8 | condition | Notification of loss
Inform us immediately after any accident, theft or damage and in any case within 7 days. For theft, file a police report straight away and do not wait for the vehicle to be found.

### [MTR-5.2] | 8 | condition | Do not repair before inspection
Do not repair the vehicle or move it from the accident site, except for safety, until we have inspected it or given written consent.

### [MTR-6.1] | 9 | claims | Documents required
Claim form, copy of the policy schedule, registration certificate, driving licence, repair estimate, and for theft the police report and both keys. For third-party injury, attach the first information report and medical records.

### [MTR-6.2] | 9 | claims | Settlement
We settle own-damage claims within 15 days of receiving the survey report and all documents. For total loss or theft, settlement is made on the basis of the IDV less the deductible.
