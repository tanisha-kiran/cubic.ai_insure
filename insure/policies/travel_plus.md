# POLICY | TRV-001 | Northstar TravelSafe Plus (synthetic sample) | travel
> Fictional policy written for the cubic.ai Insure demo. Not a real insurer or product.

### [TRV-1.1] | 2 | definition | Definitions
"Trip" means a journey outside your home country that starts and ends within the policy period. "Insured Person" means a person named on the schedule. "Pre-existing condition" means any illness or injury for which you received treatment or advice in the 24 months before the policy start date.

### [TRV-2.1] | 3 | coverage | Emergency medical expenses
We pay up to USD 100,000 per Insured Person per Trip for emergency medical treatment, hospital stay and emergency dental pain relief up to USD 500 that you need because of sudden illness or accidental injury during the Trip.

### [TRV-2.2] | 3 | coverage | Emergency medical evacuation
We pay the cost of medically necessary evacuation to the nearest suitable hospital, and repatriation to your home country where our medical adviser agrees it is necessary, up to USD 250,000.

### [TRV-3.1] | 4 | coverage | Trip cancellation
We reimburse non-refundable prepaid travel costs up to USD 5,000 if you must cancel before departure because of death, serious illness or injury of you or a close relative, a jury summons, or serious damage to your home.

### [TRV-3.2] | 4 | coverage | Trip delay
If your scheduled transport is delayed for more than 6 hours, we pay USD 60 for each full 6 hours of delay, up to USD 600, for meals and accommodation.

### [TRV-4.1] | 5 | coverage | Baggage and personal belongings
We pay up to USD 1,500 for loss of, theft of or damage to baggage and personal belongings. The most we pay for any single item, pair or set is USD 300. Laptops, cameras and phones are covered within this limit only if carried in your hand luggage.

### [TRV-4.2] | 5 | coverage | Delayed baggage
If checked baggage is delayed more than 12 hours, we pay up to USD 200 for essential clothing and toiletries.

### [TRV-5.1] | 6 | exclusion | Pre-existing conditions
We do not cover any claim arising from a pre-existing condition unless it was declared to us and accepted in writing before the Trip.

### [TRV-5.2] | 6 | exclusion | Hazardous activities
We do not cover injury or loss arising from motor racing, skydiving, bungee jumping, scuba diving deeper than 30 metres, mountaineering using ropes or climbing equipment, or any winter sport, unless the Winter Sports extension appears on your schedule.

### [TRV-5.3] | 6 | exclusion | Alcohol and drugs
We do not cover any claim where the Insured Person was under the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed drugs and this contributed to the loss or injury.

### [TRV-5.4] | 7 | exclusion | Unattended property
We do not cover loss or theft of property left unattended in a public place, in a vehicle, or in checked baggage unless it was locked in a safe or in your accommodation.

### [TRV-5.5] | 7 | exclusion | Known events and travel against advice
We do not cover claims arising from war, civil unrest, or travel to a country or region that your government has advised against all travel to at the time you booked.

### [TRV-6.1] | 8 | condition | Notification
You must tell our assistance team within 48 hours of any hospital admission and before any evacuation is arranged. If you do not, we may reduce the claim by up to 25 percent.

### [TRV-7.1] | 9 | claims | How to make a claim
Submit the claim form within 30 days of returning home. Attach the original receipts, the policy schedule, and any supporting documents listed in clause 7.2.

### [TRV-7.2] | 9 | claims | Documents required
Medical claims: doctor's report, hospital invoices and discharge summary. Baggage claims: police or airline property irregularity report within 24 hours of discovery, proof of ownership and purchase receipts. Cancellation claims: booking confirmations, cancellation invoice and a medical certificate where relevant. Delay claims: written confirmation of the delay from the carrier.

### [TRV-7.3] | 10 | limit | Excess
An excess of USD 75 applies to each claim section except trip delay and delayed baggage.
